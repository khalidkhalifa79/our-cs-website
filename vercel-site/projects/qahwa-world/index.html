The Operational Challenge

A generic article feed may not represent industry-specific topics, media formats and editorial categories clearly. The platform needs a consistent structure for maintaining subject sections and publishing different types of content.

The Engineering Response

The website combines public content presentation with an organized article-management workflow, category structures and media handling suited to coffee-sector publishing.

Engineering Considerations

The design supports a focused editorial identity while organizing publication tasks within a structured content environment.
