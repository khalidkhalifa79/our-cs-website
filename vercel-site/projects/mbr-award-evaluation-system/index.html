Project Context

Award evaluation involves participant and organization records, eligibility review, scoring, exclusions and an accessible archive. Each stage needs clear administrative control.

Project Overview

A controlled evaluation workflow for registration, eligibility, scoring, and archive management

The system organizes participant records, status tracking, eligibility review, scoring, exclusions, archives and operational reporting within a structured administrative workflow.

The Operational Challenge

Separate registration files, scoring records and eligibility checks can make reviews difficult to follow. Administrators need to distinguish qualified, incomplete and excluded cases while maintaining a consistent view of progress.

The Engineering Response

The system provides an organized workflow for participant records, status tracking, eligibility review, scoring, exclusions and archives, with reporting views to support administrative oversight.

Engineering Considerations

The solution brings related review stages into one structured administrative environment instead of treating registration, scoring and record keeping as separate tasks.
