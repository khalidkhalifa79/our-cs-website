Gaming Center Management System

Overview

A modular management system for billiard and gaming venues, from timed sessions and bookings to billing and daily revenue.

The challenge

Venues need live awareness of table and device availability, booking schedules, timed sessions and reliable payments.

The solution

One modular operations dashboard for live resources, reservations, session timers, add-ons, invoices, daily income and configurable venue modules.
