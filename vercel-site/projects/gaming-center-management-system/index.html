Gaming & Billiard Center Management System

One operational view for sessions, availability, reservations and billing.

Context Venue operations
Location Poland / International
Solution Type Resource, booking & billing platform
Platform Type Web-based management system

Project Context

Gaming and billiard venues depend on accurate resource availability, timed use, reservations, point-of-sale items, and daily financial visibility. These activities need a shared operating view rather than disconnected notes or timers.

Project Overview

A modular platform for live availability, sessions, billing, and scalable venue operations

The system is designed to bring bookable resources, timed sessions, reservations and billing into one venue workflow. Its modular concept can accommodate different activity types and related operational needs.

The Operational Challenge

Manual tracking can make it difficult to coordinate table and console use, avoid reservation conflicts, calculate charges and account for add-ons. Different activities also need to fit a consistent operating process.

The Engineering Response

The application organizes bookable resources, session timing, reservations and billing around one venue workflow. The design brings availability and transaction information into a structured operating view.

Engineering Considerations

The modular approach is intended to keep related venue activities in a coherent system rather than splitting each task into a separate tool.

Key Features

01

Live availability view

Shows the real-time status of billiard tables, snooker tables, PlayStation rooms, gaming PCs, and other bookable resources.

02

Active session timers

Allows staff to start, monitor, and end sessions with automatic usage-time calculation.

03

Reservations management

Handles reservations with start time, end time, status control, and prevention of overlapping bookings for the same resource.

04

Billing and invoices

Calculates billing based on session duration, hourly price, add-ons, discounts, and payment method.

05

Add-ons management

Products such as drinks, snacks, and extra services can be added to the session and included in the final invoice.

06

Daily revenue tracking

Provides clearer daily income visibility across sessions, products, resource usage, and completed payments.

07

Modular game types

The system can start with billiard management and expand into snooker, PlayStation, gaming PCs, and future activities.

08

Feature licensing structure

Businesses can activate only the modules they need, such as loyalty, tournaments, online booking, waiting list, or advanced reports.

Value of the Approach

The system is designed to give operators a clearer view of resource use, session progress and transaction records. No customer adoption or measured operational improvement is claimed here.
