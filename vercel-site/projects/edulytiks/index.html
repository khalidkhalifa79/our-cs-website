About the Product

Edulytiks is designed for schools that need a structured way to connect curriculum, lessons, learning outcomes, assessments, student results and analytics instead of managing those areas as disconnected records. The platform supports distinct responsibilities for platform administration, school administration, subject supervision, teachers and students while keeping school data isolated by tenant.

Project Overview

From curriculum structure to evidence-based learning analytics

The platform brings academic structure, curriculum mapping, learning outcomes, assessments, results, reporting and operational controls into one architecture. Rather than treating a test score as the final result, Edulytiks is built around outcome-level evidence so schools can understand what a student has mastered, where weaknesses remain and which academic action should come next.

The Challenge

Schools generate large amounts of academic data, but the data is often fragmented across curriculum documents, spreadsheets, assessment files and separate reporting tools. A score may show that a student performed poorly without identifying the exact learning outcome responsible for that weakness. At the same time, different school roles need different levels of authority, and multi-school software must prevent data from crossing tenant boundaries. The challenge was both academic and architectural: connect evidence to curriculum while keeping workflows clear, permissions enforceable and data securely isolated.

The Solution

Edulytiks was structured as a layered, multi-tenant platform with clear separation between domain rules, services, persistence and the web interface. Academic entities are connected so curriculum structure can flow into lessons and learning outcomes, outcomes can be mapped to assessment questions, and assessment evidence can feed analytics and reporting. Server-side authorization and tenant checks remain authoritative even when the interface hides actions unavailable to a particular role.

Business & Academic Value

The central value of Edulytiks is traceability. A school can move from curriculum to lesson, from lesson to learning outcome, from outcome to assessment evidence and from evidence to analytics. This reduces dependence on fragmented spreadsheets and gives school leadership, subject supervisors and teachers a shared academic structure while preserving role boundaries. The architecture is also designed for continued expansion without collapsing those boundaries as the platform grows.

Technical Foundation

Edulytiks uses .NET, ASP.NET Core MVC/Razor, Entity Framework Core, ASP.NET Core Identity, PostgreSQL/Npgsql and SignalR. Its architecture separates Core domain rules, Services, Data and Web layers, with durable business state in PostgreSQL, durable event intent through an Outbox pattern and realtime invalidation through SignalR. Deployment and operational workflows include health checks, migration controls, CI quality gates and security verification.
