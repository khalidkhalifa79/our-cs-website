The challenge

Schools need a connected way to manage curricula, learning outcomes, practice, assessments and actionable learning evidence across different roles.

The solution

A multi-tenant academic platform that connects lessons, curricula, outcomes, assessments and mastery analytics, with role-based workflows for administrators, supervisors, teachers and students.
