Project Context

A news platform combines a reader-facing experience with editorial processes for authors, sections, stories, media and publishing permissions. Managing both sides coherently is more demanding than maintaining a basic website.

Project Overview

A structured publishing platform for editorial control, media workflows, and intelligent section assignment

The Operational Challenge

Editors need to organize article intake across subjects and formats. Publishing workflows also need to support roles, controlled edits, media and advertising placements while keeping sections consistent and easy to navigate.

The Engineering Response

The platform brings the public news experience together with editorial tools for article management, authors, categories, media and publishing permissions. Section-classification support is described only as a workflow capability, not as a measured result.

Engineering Considerations

The structure connects publishing operations with the public reading experience, keeping editorial administration and reader-facing content within one platform.

Key Features

01

Editorial workflow

Supports structured article creation, editing, updating, and publication across multiple news sections.

02

Roles, permissions & ACP control

Provides permission-based administration with controlled access for editors and authorized users.

03

Media & social content handling

Manages images, videos, embedded social content, and advertising-related publishing materials.

04

Intelligent section classification

Analyzes article title and content to identify the most appropriate publishing section automatically.

Value of the Approach

The platform is designed to organize editorial responsibilities and content presentation in one environment. No audience growth or measured publishing-efficiency claim is made.
