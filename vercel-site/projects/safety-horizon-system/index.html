Project Context

Certificate operations bring together organizations, products, supporting information, review decisions, approvals, and formal documents. This system was designed for a certification environment requiring consistent record handling and controlled document output.

Project Overview

Structured halal certification operations with eligibility handling, approvals, and controlled document output

The platform structures certificate records and workflow states, supports preliminary checks against configured conditions, and helps route cases for administrative review or requests for missing information.

The Operational Challenge

Applications may be incomplete, ineligible for review or ready for approval. Without clear workflow rules, administrators must repeatedly check requirements and communicate missing information while keeping certificate records organized.

The Engineering Response

The platform organizes certificate records, configured preliminary checks, approval states and notifications about missing information. Document generation is connected to managed records so output can follow the defined workflow.

Engineering Considerations

The implementation places operational rules around certificate preparation while leaving final approval authority with the appropriate administrator.

Key Features

01

Multi-certificate workflows

Supports A, B, and annual certificate workflows inside one structured operational system.

02

Eligibility & preliminary issuance logic

Evaluates whether certificate data meets the required conditions before preliminary approval and issuance.

03

Approval & notification control

Automatically submits eligible certificates to the administrator and notifies clients when required information is missing.

04

PDF & QR document output

Generates formal certificate PDFs with office-specific information, branding, and QR-enabled output.

Value of the Approach

The workflow is intended to make certificate preparation and administrative review easier to follow. It does not imply statutory accreditation, regulatory approval or certification authority by OUR-CS.
