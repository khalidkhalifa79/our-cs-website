The Operational Challenge

Repeated data entry, duplicate materials and inconsistent classification can create avoidable editorial work. Unstructured archives make past materials harder to locate, while manual reporting can obscure workflow patterns.

The Engineering Response

The workflow organizes source links and article metadata, checks for duplicates, classifies content and records defined editorial activity. Reporting views present the selected measures for administrative review without publishing sensitive internal metrics.

Engineering Considerations

The system combines structured intake and archiving with reporting views, supporting editorial operations without replacing editorial judgement.
